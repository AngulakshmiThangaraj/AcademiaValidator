About Authenticity Validator for Academia

Smart India Hackathon SIH25029 Academic Credential Protection Platform

SIH25029 Problem Statement & Innovation

Academic marksheets and certificates are frequently targeted for forgery, alteration of subject marks, CGPA manipulation, and fake registration numbers. Traditional verification relies on manual calls or slow postal registry verification.

Authenticity Validator for Academia provides a complete end-to-end automated solution combining Bilingual Tamil/English OCR, MobileNetV2 Deep Learning AI, ELA Compression Forensics, and Cryptographic Board Registry Lookups into an instant 100-point verification engine.
