9-Stage Forensic Verification Workflow

Comprehensive step-by-step breakdown of how documents are ingested, parsed, analyzed, and scored

1

Document Image Preprocessing

Resolution normalization, adaptive contrast enhancement (PIL.ImageEnhance), sharpening filter, and binarization thresholding to prepare physical marksheets for OCR.

2

Bilingual Tamil + English OCR

Engineered for Indian state board marksheets (`eng+tam` traineddata). Extracts candidate name, register number, certificate ID, total marks, and passing session with confidence scores.

3

PyTorch MobileNetV2 AI Forgery Classifier

Deep learning vision model evaluates overall image texture, seal boundaries, font baseline alignment, and tampered mark patterns (Genuine Probability vs Forgery Risk).

4

Error Level Analysis (ELA) Compression Forensics

Re-saves image at known compression quality (95%) and computes error difference heatmap. Spliced or edited mark digits exhibit higher error levels highlighted in red.

5

Cryptographic QR Signature Decoding

Locates and decodes embedded 2D QR codes using OpenCV/PyZbar. Parses candidate metadata and computes SHA-256 hash of the normalized QR payload string.

6

Official Board Registry Matching

Executes multi-parameter lookup in SQLite registry database using Certificate ID, Register Number, Student Name (fuzzy string matching), and Date of Birth.

7

SHA-256 Document Fingerprint Integrity

Computes cryptographic SHA-256 checksum of raw image bytes to verify against tamper-proof registered document hashes.

8

Multi-Factor Authenticity Scoring

Weights individual component scores (OCR 20%, Registry Match 15%, QR Signature 15%, ELA 15%, AI Model 20%, SHA-256 15%) into a deterministic 100-point score.

9

Explainable Verdict & Report Generation

Outputs clear decision verdict (`VERIFIED`, `SUSPICIOUS`, or `INVALID`) along with itemized field comparison table and downloadable PDF Forensic Verification Certificate.

Multi-Factor Security Architecture

Zero-trust verification framework combining computer vision, AI, and cryptography

Why Single-Factor Verification Fails

A printed QR code can be copied onto a forged marksheet, while an authentic document can have individual mark digits altered. Relying solely on QR codes or visual inspection leaves critical vulnerabilities.

Multi-Factor Security Formula:
Authenticity Score = AI_Model(20%) + OCR(20%) + Registry(15%) + QR(15%) + ELA(15%) + SHA256(15%)

Complementing DigiLocker & NAD

While DigiLocker and National Academic Depository (NAD) store authoritative native digital documents, millions of admissions and job applications involve physical paper copies or scanned images.

Authenticity Validator for Academia acts as a forensic verification layer specifically designed to audit physical, scanned, or photographed marksheet images.

SIH25029 Problem Statement & Innovation

Academic marksheets and certificates are frequently targeted for forgery, alteration of subject marks, CGPA manipulation, and fake registration numbers. Traditional verification relies on manual calls or slow postal registry verification.

Authenticity Validator for Academia provides a complete end-to-end automated solution combining Bilingual Tamil/English OCR, MobileNetV2 Deep Learning AI, ELA Compression Forensics, and Cryptographic Board Registry Lookups into an instant 100-point verification engine.
